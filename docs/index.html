WHAT YOU GET

Built for people who film themselves

A prompter, a camera, and a script editor that understand each other — so a shoot day is write, read, record, done.

Floating overlay

Run the prompter over any app — your camera, Instagram, TikTok, a video call. Drag it, pinch to resize, fade it, or collapse it to a bubble between takes.

Built-in 4K studio

Record with the script hovering under the lens. Tap to focus, pinch to zoom, exposure, torch, framing grid, and 16:9 / 4:3 / 1:1 crops.

Voice sync

The prompter listens and keeps your current line in the reading band. Speed up, slow down, pause to think — the script follows you, not the other way around.

Takes that stay organised

Every recording is linked back to the script it was read from, with thumbnails, duration and quality — so you always know which take is which.

Import anything

Bring in .txt, .docx, .pdf and .md. Markdown headings become prompter sections automatically, so an outlined document arrives ready to film.

Made for real rigs

Mirroring for beam-splitter glass, volume keys and Bluetooth clickers for speed and play/pause, and a start-delay countdown to get in position.

SCRIPT MARKERS

Write it like a script, not a wall of text

Markers tell the prompter what to do — and never count as words you speak, so your estimated read time and voice sync stay accurate.

## Intro Hey everyone — welcome back. [[pause]] Today we're unboxing the Flux 2. [[b-roll: slow pan across the desk]] ## The verdict It's the readout that changes things. [[pause 3s]] Let me show you.
  • ## Section

    Section headerShows as a heading, and gives you a jump point you can skip back to while filming.

  • [[pause]]

    Stop and waitScrolling halts when the line reaches the reading band. Tap to carry on — good for ad-libs.

  • [[pause 3s]]

    Timed holdSame, but counts down and resumes on its own.

  • [[b-roll: …]]

    Stage directionA dim note to yourself — camera moves, props, reminders. Shown, never spoken.
