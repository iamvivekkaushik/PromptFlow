Stop looking away from the lens. PromptFlow puts your script right where you're filming — floating over any camera app, or inside its own 4K recording studio — and scrolls at the pace you actually speak.

FLOATING OVERLAY
Run the prompter over any app: your camera, Instagram, TikTok, a video call. Drag it anywhere, pinch to resize, dial in the opacity, or collapse it to a tiny bubble between takes. Your favorite camera app keeps doing the filming — PromptFlow just feeds you the lines.

BUILT-IN 4K STUDIO
Prefer an all-in-one? Record straight from PromptFlow with the script hovering under the lens, so your eyes stay on camera. Up to 4K at 30 or 60 fps, 16:9 / 4:3 / 1:1 framing, front or rear camera, live mic level — takes land in your gallery, ready to edit.

VOICE SYNC
The prompter listens as you read and keeps the current line in the guide band. Speed up, slow down, pause to think — the script follows you, not the other way around. Prefer a fixed pace? Set words-per-minute and adjust live while recording.

YOUR SCRIPTS, ANY SOURCE
Write in the built-in editor or import .txt, .docx, .pdf, and .md files. Scripts keep their reading progress, estimated duration, and a recorded/draft status, so a shoot day stays organized.

MADE FOR REAL RIGS
• Mirror text horizontally or vertically for beam-splitter teleprompter glass
• Volume keys, Bluetooth clickers, and keyboards control play/pause and speed
• Start-delay countdown so you can get in position
• Tap or double-tap to pause anywhere

TYPE IT YOUR WAY
Adjustable size, line spacing, and weight, a library of fonts (plus your own .ttf/.otf files), and text colors tuned for readability over a live viewfinder.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No cloud. No analytics. Scripts and settings live only on your device, and recordings save to your own gallery. The app doesn't even use the internet — except to fetch fonts you pick.
