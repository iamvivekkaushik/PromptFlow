PromptFlow is an on-device teleprompter for creators.

• Script library — write, import (.txt, .docx, .pdf, .md) and organize scripts
• In-app studio — record up to 4K with the prompter floating over your camera preview
• Floating overlay — run the prompter over any camera app
• Voice sync — scrolling follows your speaking pace
• Full typography control — size, weight, fonts, colors, mirroring for beam-splitter rigs
• Hardware remotes — volume keys, Bluetooth clickers and keyboards
• Private by design — everything stays on your device, no account needed
